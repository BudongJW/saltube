1min_scientist uploader

A personal, open-source command-line tool

This tool uploads short-form science videos, produced by its operator, to that operator's own TikTok drafts. It does not publish posts — the account holder completes and publishes each post inside the TikTok app.

It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by TikTok or ByteDance.
